# Treasure chests

Three chests, **common**, **rare** and **legendary**, that stand on one ground tile. Each is drawn **NE and SE**, **shut** (one frame) and **opening** (an animation). They get bigger and richer with rarity:
common is a small banded wooden box (24 px wide), rare is a mahogany chest on feet with teal steel trim and a sapphire lock (30 px), legendary is violet lacquer trimmed in gold with a crested lid, gems and claw feet (32 px, a full tile).
On the Assets page they are the **Chests** category.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `chest-RARITY-ne.png`, `chest-RARITY-se.png` | The chest shut. One frame |
| `chest-RARITY-ne-open.png`, `chest-RARITY-se-open.png` | The chest opening. 8 frames |
| `chests.json` | Every file, the cell, the anchor and the frame rate, for the app to read |

`RARITY` is `common`, `rare` or `legendary`. Each sheet is one row of **48 x 52** frames in the shared palette's colour roles, so it recolours with the rest of the game. The cell is wider than a tile (32) because the lid and the light swing out past it.

## Placing one

The **anchor is (24, 40)**: the middle of the ground tile, where a character stands. Draw it after the ground and under anything standing on that tile:

```js
const gx = mapOffsetX + 16 * (x - y), gy = mapOffsetY + 8 * (x + y);            // the ground tile's top left, as in the buildings README
ctx.drawImage(sheet, frame * 48, 0, 48, 52, gx + 16 - 24, gy + 8 - 40, 48, 52);  // the anchor (24, 40) on the tile's middle (16, 8)
```

## Facings

**NE** has the lock and hasp on the wall that runs NE-SW (the right-hand face as the camera sees it, lit) and **SE** on the wall that runs NW-SE (the left-hand face, shaded). **NW and SW** are those two flipped
about the anchor, as for the characters. The lid hinges on the back, away from the front, so it always opens toward the camera's far side.

## Opening

- Swap the shut sprite for the opening sheet when the chest is opened. Play it **once** at **10 frames a second** and **hold the last frame**; it does not loop.
- The frames: shut, a crack of light, the lid lifting, half open, wide, a little past upright, settling, open. The first frame is the shut chest, so the swap does not jump.
- The last frame is the open chest, the picture to keep drawing. Its inside glows and a few sparkles hang over it.
- Light and sparkle colours follow rarity: cream, cyan, gold. The treasure inside is a few coins, gold and a gem, and a heap of gold and jewels.

## Rebuilding these files

```bash
python3 tools/make_assets.py --png-dir sprites/assets        # writes this whole folder, and the Chests category on the Assets page
python3 tools/make_chest.py --preview previews/chests.png     # every chest, shut and opening, enlarged
```

They are generated by code (a small 3D model of a hollow body, a hinged lid and treasure, drawn in the ground tiles' projection), so change `tools/make_chest.py` (`SPECS` for a rarity, `FRAMES` for the opening) and rebuild instead of editing the PNGs.
